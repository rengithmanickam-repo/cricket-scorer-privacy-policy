Howzat - Counter

Cricket Umpire Score Counter

About

Howzat - Counter is a simple, fast cricket scoring counter built for umpires, scorers, and anyone keeping track of a match. Score every delivery with a single tap — runs, wickets, wides, no-balls, leg-byes, and byes.

FAQ

How do I reset the score?

Go to the Settings tab and tap "Reset Log & Counters". This will clear all ball history, overs, wickets, and totals.

Is my data saved?

Yes. Your scoring session is saved automatically on your device and persists between app launches. No internet connection is required.

Can I undo a mistake?

Yes. Tap the Undo button on the Home screen to remove the last delivery. You can also tap Redo to restore it.

How do I log a run out on a wide?

Tap Wd, then tap 0–6 for runs off the wide, then tap W to record the wicket on the same delivery.

Can I change balls per over?

Yes. Go to Settings > Match Limits and adjust the balls per over, max wickets, and target overs.
